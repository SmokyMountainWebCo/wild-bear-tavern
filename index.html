Inside Wild Bear Inn

Bavaria found a home on the Parkway.

Most visitors come to Pigeon Forge for lights, shows, and a fast dinner. Wild Bear Tavern is the exception people drive back for. Roadfood called it legendary. Locals send guests here for schnitzel that actually tastes German, and for a breakfast that can fuel Dollywood and still leave room for apple strudel.

The room is dark wood and mountain quiet. Service is the kind that remembers your beer. The kitchen runs three meals a day: buttermilk pancakes until late morning, then bratwurst, goulash, Jäger schnitzel, and Opa’s pork shank after five.

World of Westgate members save up to 25 percent. The patio takes the dog. The kitchen will walk a gluten-free table through the menu. Reservations are on OpenTable when the Parkway is loud.
